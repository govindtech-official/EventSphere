Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

EventSphere is a student web-design project that lets you browse sample events and register for them. This policy explains what information the website uses and where it is kept.

1. Information we use

  • Google account details: when you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the site. We only request these basic profile details.
  • Registration details: the phone number, event and number of tickets you enter in the registration form.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme choice.

2. Where your information is stored

Everything is saved only in your own browser using Local Storage. EventSphere has no server or database, and we do not send, sell or share your information with anyone.

3. Third-party services

The website loads a few resources from other services, which may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when your browser requests them:

  • Google Identity Services – handles Sign in with Google (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Fonts – provides the Poppins font.
  • Unsplash – provides the event images.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the website.

4. Cookies

EventSphere does not set its own cookies. Google may set cookies on its own domains to keep you signed in to your Google account.

5. Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from the profile menu.
  • Cancel any booking from My Bookings.
  • Delete all data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Remove EventSphere's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

6. Children

EventSphere is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
